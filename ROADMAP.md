# Roadmap

A short outlook, without dates. graineo is a spare-time project; plans change. Details and discussion live in the [issues](https://github.com/graineo/graineo/issues).

## Next

- **Public test suite and CI:** a fixed set of freely licensed sample photos, one command to check a build (`graineo-dev` self-test), and automatic checks for pull requests.
- **More GPU work:** Bayer demosaicing (RCD) and X-Trans direction decisions on the GPU with the same result as the CPU; film simulation on the GPU for very large pictures (processed in tiles); the spektrafilm diffusion filters computed exactly on the GPU; masks and looks on the GPU for large regions.
- **Windows:** from experimental to a regular build.
- **Translations:** French, Spanish and Japanese are machine-translated; review by native speakers is welcome.
- **Release channels:** stable and beta, “What's new” inside the app.

## Ideas

- **Measure your own camera look:** build a camera look for your own camera and picture style from your RAW + JPEG pairs, with a quality indicator.
- **Export of all edits** as XMP sidecars for the whole library in one step, documented as “take your data with you”.

Suggestions are welcome as feature requests.
